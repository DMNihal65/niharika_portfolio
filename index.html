Editor's letter

Hello, I'm Niharika.

Right now I'm in my final year of Information Science and Engineering at Basaveshwara Engineering College in Bagalkot. I want to work in data analytics and data engineering, and I'm learning both the best way I know: by building things.

So far that means Python, SQL and databases, small backends in FastAPI and PostgreSQL, and a lot of time in Pandas cleaning data until it can actually answer a question. Next on my list is the bigger stuff: cloud tools and Spark, used properly and at scale.

“Good data is like a good outfit. Nobody sees the work, only that it looks right.”

Outside class I've been a class representative, and I helped run the IEEE WIE Congress as a volunteer. Outside data, I dance, and I follow fashion and styling more closely than I'd probably admit in an interview.

Two things I built

Feature 01 · p.06

The meeting that takes its own notes

Upload a meeting recording and get back a transcript, a short summary, the decisions that were made and who is doing what next.

  • Speech to text with OpenAI Whisper.
  • Google Gemini turns the transcript into a summary, key decisions, action items and takeaways.
  • A FastAPI and PostgreSQL backend keeps the meetings, transcripts and users organised, so you can find a meeting again later.
  • Whisper
  • Gemini
  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL

Feature 02 · p.08

Reading a leaf

A farmer-friendly app: take a photo of a crop, find out which pest is on it, and get advice on what to do about it.

  • A convolutional neural network (CNN) picks out the pest from the photo.
  • It suggests organic and chemical treatments, with safety guidelines.
  • It checks WeatherAPI data and warns when wind would carry the spray where it shouldn't go.
  • CNN
  • Python
  • WeatherAPI
  • Web and mobile
